# A race into the inner post, and the run-up slip (2026-11-17)

Layout E (`docs/mazes.md`, the staircase of six curves), firmware of 11-17
(`practice`, develop b2ef029). Log `software/logs/2026-11-17_21-48-02.log`,
recordings `software/calib_data/2026-11-17_2*_run.csv`.

## 1. The crash

- Race 2.4 (`FAST` 800, `CURVE` 300), route `2R1R1L1R1L1L2`, right after a
  clean search (31 actions, no "!!").
- `route 9 cells, 6 curves: end=WALL dist=1191.59/1193.43mm v=800/300
  IR(FL=59 FR=93) pace=87% err=3.77mm/5.34deg`, then `!! FAST: BLOCKED at
  (3,1)N`, `End: FAILED`.
- The user: it arrived at the last curve (left, east to north, in (3,0))
  far off and hit the curve's inner post. No telemetry in that run.
- The wheels were dirty, the floor a little too. The user cleaned the
  wheels; the same race then ran clean twice (below). Most likely cause:
  wheel slip, the encoders counting more than the robot moved, so the
  curves started early and cut the inside (as the OSHWDEM failure,
  `oshwdem2026.md`).

## 2. How it was narrowed down (all `CAL RUN` recordings, `ratatron calib`)

`par` = the encoder heading at which the first straight runs parallel to
the walls (0 = the encoders agree with the walls); `in` = the lateral error
entering the last straight (+ = left = the inside of the last curve).

| Run | FAST/CURVE | ACCEL | start | par | in | PWM limit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2.4 | 400/300 | 3000 | by hand | +1.1 | -8.9 | 0 |
| 2.4 | 800/300 | 3000 | by hand | -1.0 | +2.6 | 1 |
| 2.5 | 900/400 | 3000 | after the return | -5.9 | +22.7 | 7 |
| 2.6 | 900/400 | 3000 | after the return | -6.5 | +10.1 | 4 |
| 2.6 | 900/400 | 3000 | by hand, straight | -5.0 | +6.0 | 5 |
| 2.6 | 900/400 | 2000 | by hand, straight | -0.4 | -0.6 | 0 |
| 2.6 | 900/400 | 2000 | after the return | -0.4 | +10.4 | 0 |

- At 900 with `ACCEL` 3000 the encoder heading ends 5-6.5 deg off the
  walls during the run-up (it grows with the speed, 0-100 mm), while the
  lateral error stays within 5 mm: the robot runs straight, the encoders
  say it turned. A wheel slipping (PWM at the limit), not a crooked start:
  placed straight by hand it was the same (-5.0).
- The curves then take that error with them: they keep at most 2 deg of
  the centring's offset (the 11-17 change, `df03938`) and throw away the
  rest, which here is real. In 2.5 the staircase ran ~4 deg rotated:
  lateral -10.5 and -32.9 mm between curves, +22.7 at the last straight
  (the inner side, 40 mm of clearance in a curve). A near miss.
- `ACCEL` 2000: no slip, the same time to the goal (3430 vs 3433 ms: on this
  route the curves set the pace).

Along the track too: the races' end found by the front IR against the
route's planned length (`path.c`): at `ACCEL` 3000 and 900 the wall was
+23, +30 and +30 mm further than the encoders said (+30 is the
`FRONT_LATE_MAX_MM` clamp: the robot stopped ~20 mm short, FL 103 instead of
83), ~50 mm overcounted in one run-up; at 2000, -10 and -1. Rolling (layout
F, 34 m of laps) the encoders lose only ~5 mm: slips make the error.

## 3. Reproduce

Layout E, a search, then `MODE 2 6`, `CAL RUN`, `START`, `CAL DUMP`;
`ratatron calib` on the CSV: the first straight's "parallel to the walls at
encoder heading". With `ACCEL 2000` before `START`: about 0.

## 4. Fix and what is open

- Fixed (`fba2b1f`, next flash): a route's run (races, `CAL`) accelerates at
  most `RACE_ACCEL_MM_S2` 2000; the search keeps `ACCEL` 3000 (at 2000 its
  legs would decide each cell before having its walls in view: the host
  test of the leg window fails).
- Open: the 2 deg clamp of the curves (`df03938`) was chosen in the
  simulator, which does not slip; with any real encoder heading error it
  discards a true offset. To rehearse in the simulator with an encoder bias.
- Opening the curves (the user's question: they fail on the inside): no
  room. With the body's outline (72 mm wide, 45 ahead of the axle, 29
  behind) the curve has 40.0 mm to the inner post and 39.4 to the outer
  walls; the widest curve that still fits consecutive cells (R 85, ramp 8)
  gains 0.1 mm. Moving it outwards trades clearance 1:1. The inside hits
  come from along-track error (curves started early), which `CAL LAPS`
  measures.

## 5. The limit in the simulator (2026-11-18)

The 523 real mazes (micromouseonline classic), the maps of one search each
(`ratatron sim batch --maps`), races 2.4 then 2.6, the firmware with
`RACE_ACCEL_MM_S2` 2000 against the same at 3000 (the races as before
`fba2b1f`). The simulator does not slip by itself, so this shows the cost:

| race | failed 2000 / 3000 | median time to the goal 2000 / 3000 |
|---|---|---|
| 2.4 (800/300) | 29 / 38 of 523 | 19.42 / 18.80 s (+0.62 s, +3.3 %), 476 mazes |
| 2.6 (900/480) | 68 / 88 of 523 | 14.45 / 13.99 s (+0.46 s, +3.3 %), 423 mazes |

Hull touches 111 / 196. The limit costs ~3 % of the run to the goal (about
half a second on a 16x16) and fails less even without slip: braking at 2000
leaves the curves a calmer entry.

With the slip modelled (`--slip 6`: the run-up of 11-17, the first straight
parallel at -6 deg; 105 of the mazes, 2.6 alone): 3000 with the slip fails 31,
2000 without it 16; the median to the goal 12.92 against 13.29 s.
